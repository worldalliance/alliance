# Action idea requirements

Human-editable working list; it may not be exhaustive.

- Ideas must be nonpartisan. Nonpartisan civic and policy actions are okay.
- Ideas must relate to combating extreme poverty, environmental destruction, democratic decline, or dangerous technologies, or improve the Alliance itself (a meta action).
- Members must be able to participate on their own time and from anywhere in the world, without having to show up at a particular location or time.
- Soft preference: member participation should be engaging, easy, and accessible.
