# Checkout Optimization: 5 Ways to Minimize Form Fields in Checkout

Source: <https://baymard.com/research-articles/checkout-flow-average-form-fields>

Retrieved: 2026-11-06

© Baymard Institute 2026. Excerpts only, quoted for the guidelines items that cite this page; the page itself has the rest.

---

> - The average checkout flow in 2024 is 5.1 steps long and contains 11.3 form fields — and 17% of users have abandoned due to checkout complexity ([#GC050](https://baymard.com/premium/quant-insights/GC050?time_period=March+2026))
> - Reducing the number of fields users must contend with will improve checkout UX
>
> Therefore, to further streamline the checkout process for the majority of users, **hide _“Address Line 2”_** behind a link.
>
> Furthermore, by applying the same reasoning to additional, optional fields (e.g., _“Company Name”_, _“home/work/cell phone number”_, _“Fax”_, etc.) — hiding them behind links — and by providing a [“Fully Automatic Address Lookup” feature](https://baymard.com/blog/automatic-address-lookup), you can now present users with only the fields they **absolutely need** in order to effectively complete their checkout.
