> - **1.2 User-Generated Content**
>
> Apps that enable users to create, generate, upload, share, or otherwise contribute content present particular challenges, including intellectual property infringement, nonconsensual intimate imagery, and anonymous bullying. This includes social media, user-generated content platforms, and AI content creation apps. To prevent abuse, such apps must include:
>
> - A mechanism to report offensive content and timely responses to concerns
> - The ability to block abusive users from the service
> - **(v) Account Sign-In:** If your app doesn’t include significant account-based features, let people use it without a login. If your app supports account creation, you must also [offer account deletion within the app](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion). Apps may not require users to enter personal information to function, except when directly relevant to the core functionality of the app or required by law. If your core app functionality is not related to a specific social network (such as Facebook, WeChat, Weibo, X, etc.), you must provide access without a login or via another mechanism. Pulling basic profile information, sharing to the social network, or inviting friends to use the app are not considered core app functionality. The app must also include a mechanism to revoke social network credentials and disable data access between the app and social network from within the app. An app may not store credentials or tokens to social networks off of the device and may only use such credentials or tokens to directly connect to the social network from the app itself while the app is in use.
