- `findActiveUsersForRoster` loads users, contract events and away ranges as three column-only queries joined in JS instead of TypeORM relation loading, which cost several times the CPU for the same rows (about 4x for 690 users locally). Contract events load as raw rows with their `userId` column, since selecting the `user` relation joins and hydrates a `User` per event, most of the remaining CPU. Its other callers (cohort decision pass, `decideOpenAction`, cohort divergence, action relations) read only `id`, `date` and `type` of contract events (`isSettled`, the contract predicates) and `startDate`/`endDate` of away ranges. Both loaders exclude soft-deleted rows: TypeORM's query-strategy relation load passes `withDeleted` from the find options, false by default. A comparison on the staging snapshot found identical users, contract events and away ranges for all 690 users. (agent decision)
