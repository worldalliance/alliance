# User-origin requirements

- An ongoing task assigned optionally to a late joiner belongs behind onboarding for that member, without lowering its priority for everyone.
- Admins must be able to interleave explicitly optional tasks with required tasks, including offering a break after demanding required work.
- Late-joiner optional tasks should go to the end of the list. They should stay there after onboarding finishes; the user favors the simpler rule over tracking onboarding completion.
- Onboarding has no privileged position.
- Surface everything that can appear on the home page in the ordering interface, with badges explaining why it is included, especially tasks available after their deadline.

# Accepted agent proposals

The user approved these agent-authored proposals; approval does not make their design agent-independent.

- Apply the late-joiner exception only to tasks made optional for that member by joining late. An action explicitly marked optional by an admin retains its chosen position.
- Apply the exception to multiple ongoing assignments and to returning members who re-sign mid-task.
- Include scheduled items and staff previews even when ordinary members cannot see them yet, without requiring an actual member to currently qualify.
- Keep unscheduled drafts visible for advance ordering. Explain that they are not ordinary member home tasks and mark staff-preview availability separately.
- Preserve platform layouts: general updates stay separate from tasks on web and mixed into the sequence on mobile. Explain that cross-type ordering affects mobile. Feed posts, activity, and progress displays remain outside this ordering screen.
- Passing a deadline leaves a still-visible task in its admin-chosen position; show its post-deadline availability in the ordering interface.
- Keep follow-ups after all task cards, including late-joiner tasks. Show them in a separate read-only section of the ordering screen, with their parent task, availability dates, and repeatable/fixed-position explanation. Preserve their existing repeated-submission behavior.
- Final approval: admin order governs onboarding and required tasks as well as explicitly optional tasks; late-joiner optional tasks alone move last among task cards.
