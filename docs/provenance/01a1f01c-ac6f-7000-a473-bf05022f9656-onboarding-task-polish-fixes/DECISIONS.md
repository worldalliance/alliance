# Decisions

- **`OutputRenderer` has a `plain` mode** on web and mobile that drops the grey card, inner cards and labels and shows each answer as text and its uploads as thumbnails (`plainAnswer`, shared): line breaks kept, a list's rows one per line with each row's shown answers comma-joined, and the uploads in a list's rows as thumbnails after its text. Unanswered fields are skipped instead of printing "No response", and an answer of a field kind the build doesn't know shows as its formatted text, since an installed app can receive a newer form. A list shows as text rather than its row cards because the requirement asks for no inner cards or labels. (agent decision, from review findings `strings-as-files`, `list-as-count`, `list-uploads-dropped` and `unknown-kind-throws`; supersedes the matching entry in `01a1efbf-56c3-7000-b770-98e1ac50e663-onboarding-task-polish/DECISIONS.md`)
- **A header after the first item keeps the space above it (`pt-4`) on the plain completion card**, as on the full card, so plain mode drops only the card chrome and labels the requirement names. (agent decision, from review finding `header-spacing`)
- **Unselected wheel numbers are `zinc-600`**, the nearest grey to the design's that meets WCAG AA's 4.5:1 for text: about 7:1 on the `zinc-100` wheel, where the design's `zinc-500` is about 4.4:1. Supersedes the `zinc-500` entry in `01a1efbf-56c3-7000-b770-98e1ac50e663-onboarding-task-polish/DECISIONS.md`. (agent decision, from review finding `unselected-contrast`)
- **The dark number is the row under the band, updated as the column scrolls**, not only once the scroll settles. (agent decision, from review finding `stale-selected-colour`)
- **The 16px gap under the question is the label's bottom margin**, not the wheel's top margin, so a hidden label leaves no gap. `RenderLabel` takes the margin only when something above the wheel shows, the question or "Optional": on mobile it renders nothing otherwise, output views included, and on web, where an empty label still renders, it drops the margin. (agent decision, from review findings `hidden-label-gap`, `web-optional-gap`, `mobile-output-gap` and `duplicated-visibility`)
- **A form whose `atLeastOneOnWith` names anything but another toggle in it doesn't save** (`validateFormSchema`, on the server and in the builder). Such a toggle would be required with nothing to turn on, so deleting a partner fails loudly at save instead of silently forcing the other toggle on. (agent decision, from review finding `dangling-partner`)
- **Answering a paired toggle clears the errors its answer resolves**: the error of any toggle it turns on, and the required error of each toggle paired with it or with the toggle turned on that was off and required by its pairing, each only once it validates against the new answers (`answerField`, shared by the web and mobile form state). A partner still invalid for its own reason, such as being required, keeps its error, and so does a partner the pairing didn't require, whose error may come from a custom validator the form state doesn't rerun. (agent decision, from review findings `stale-partner-error`, `partner-over-clear`, `transitive-stale-error` and `clears-unrelated-errors`)
