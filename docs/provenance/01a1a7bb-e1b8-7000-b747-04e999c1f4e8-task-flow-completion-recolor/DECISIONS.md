- **Wide is Tailwind's `md` and up.** The user's "desktop (wide breakpoints)" and "vertical breakpoints" are read as either side of `md` (`48rem`, 768px), the breakpoint where the side nav shows beside the flow and the panel stops covering the whole window. `useCompletionPhase` checks it with `useMediaQuery`.
- **"Yes" to keeping the fade-in is read as the recommended option.** Asked whether the fade-in should stay, with options for a 200ms or a 500ms fade back to white, the user answered "yes"; the recommended option, a 200ms fade back, is the one built. Making the fade back 500ms means pointing `greenMotion` at `flowMotion.success` both ways.
- **Three phases.** On a narrow screen, `useCompletionPhase` in `TaskFlowComplete.tsx` moves the screen from `Arriving` (white, dark title) to `Green` two frames after it first renders, so the fade-in starts from a painted white frame, and to `Settled` once the fade-in and the hold have passed. On a wide screen it returns `Settled` from the first render and starts no timers. Leaving the `Complete` stage clears the timers and resets the phase.
- **The nav's tone follows the phase.** `TaskFlow` maps the phase to the `ImmersiveTone` it passes to `useImmersiveNavbar` through `COMPLETION_TONE`, so the nav is never green on a wide screen. Below `md` the panel covers the nav and top band, so their green no longer shows at any width; they keep their `flowMotion.recolor` transitions.
- **Each direction has its own duration.** A CSS transition takes the duration of the state it's heading to, so `greenMotion(green)` in web's `flowMotion.ts` gives the screen `flowMotion.success` (500ms) on its way into green and `flowMotion.recolor` (200ms) on its way out. The 2s hold is `SUCCESS_HOLD_MS` beside it rather than in `shared/forms/flowStyle.ts`, since mobile doesn't hold.
- **Resizing mid-screen.** Widening the window to `md` while the screen is green settles it at once; narrowing it below `md` on the completion screen starts the green from the beginning. Both are rare enough not to special-case.
- **Closing is unchanged.** Closing during the green turns everything plain and fades the screen out over 200ms, as before; closing later fades the already-white screen to the panel's white, so the panel shrinks empty either way.
- **The answers card gets a border on white.** It's `border-zinc-200` whenever the screen is white, as the card under "View task" on the action page has, and transparent on green.
- **The bottom fade follows the background.** TN-13's gradient goes from white to green and back with the screen. Tailwind 4 registers `--tw-gradient-from` as a `<color>` property, so it transitions with the rest.
- **Reduced motion keeps the timing.** The phases change on the same schedule; only the fades are skipped.
