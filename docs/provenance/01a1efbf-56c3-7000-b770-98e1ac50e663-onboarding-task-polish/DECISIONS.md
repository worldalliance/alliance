# Decisions

Supersedes, in `01a1d17d-ac6e-7000-bf2f-5173bdd6d227-onboarding-profile-mapping/DECISIONS.md`, the entry making "at least one notification toggle on" form configuration through the email toggle's required-if formula.

- **"At least one on" is a toggle setting, `atLeastOneOnWith`, naming another toggle.** Turning either toggle of a pair off while the other is off turns the other on (`pairedToggleToTurnOn`, called from the one answer-write helper the web and mobile form state share). The toggle that names its partner is required while the partner is not on (`pairedToggleRequired`, inside `isFieldConditionallyRequired`), so the server, which strips hidden answers first, refuses both off, and a hidden partner (the text toggle for a member without a phone number) leaves the named toggle required. Set in the builder as "Keep at least one on with". Chosen over deriving the flip from required-if formulas, which would also turn on acknowledgement-style toggles whose formula reads another toggle being off. The required-if formula on the email toggle is no longer needed. (agent decision)
- **A hidden partner is never turned on; the toggle the member turned off stays on instead.** A hidden toggle has no answer to submit, so turning it on would leave the member with no channel on. When every partner is hidden, as for the email toggle of a member with no phone number, the tap leaves the toggle on, which keeps one on at all times instead of failing on Complete. (agent decision)
- **Toggles never show the "Optional" prefix.** A toggle always answers, off included, so "Optional" says nothing about it. "Required" on a toggle still means it must be on. (agent decision)
- **The wheel's sizes live in `timeWheelLayout`** (`shared/forms/timeWheel.ts`): 36px rows, a 120px wheel, 56px columns, scaled by the font scale on mobile, with the selected row centred and the fade at half the space above it, so the neighbouring rows stay readable at the shorter height. The gap under the question is 16px on both platforms. (agent decision)
- **Unselected wheel numbers are `zinc-500`**, the design's grey. On the `zinc-100` wheel that is about 4.4:1, just under WCAG AA's 4.5:1 for text, before the edge fade lightens it further; the selected value, which screen readers announce, is `zinc-900`. (agent judgement)
- **The completion card's plain look is a `plain` prop** on `TaskAnswersCard` and `OutputRenderer`, set only by the completion screen. It drops the grey card, inner cards and labels, renders each answer as text (images stay thumbnails), and skips unanswered fields instead of printing "No response". The photo grows from 32px to 48px with a 6px radius, mobile's existing `larger` profile image size. (agent decision)
