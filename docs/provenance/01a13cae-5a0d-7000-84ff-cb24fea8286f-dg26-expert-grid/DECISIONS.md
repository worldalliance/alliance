`ExpertGrid` renders `FEATURED_PEOPLE` in order. Each person is one cell.

`ExpertGrid` is separate from the old `PersonRow`. That row placed the face beside the name; each cell here stacks the face, then the name, then the title (`role`).

Each column is 125 pixels, the face size, so the name and title wrap under the photo. The list is four columns from the `sm` breakpoint and two columns below it.

The expert block is the first content inside the timeline section, above the phase list. From `lg`, the sentence and the grid share a row and the sentence is vertically centered against the grid. Below `lg` the sentence is above the grid. The grid stays on the right at every width. Each name and title stays centered under its face.
